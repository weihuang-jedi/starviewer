NV for CAM:
  • CAM-FV
    • Swith on/off NCL to switch images generated from NCL/OpenGL
    • Swith Flat button on/off to view image flat or on sphere
  • CAM-SE
    • Swith Flat button on/off to view image flat or on sphere
    • Click Here to play a short movie of bumped TMQ.

    NV for POP:
  • POP
    • Swith on/off Flat button to view images on sphere or plane.

    NV for WRF:
  • WRF
    • Swith on/off NCL to switch images generated from NCL/OpenGL
    • Swith bump button on to view image bumped

    NV for MPAS:
  • MPAS
    • Swith on/off flat to view images on sphere or plane.

    NV for RADAR:
  • RADAR
    • User Z-slider to view different sweeps.
